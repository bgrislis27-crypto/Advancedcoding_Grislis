The Backrooms

A first-person maze. The halls are yellow, the lights fail on their own, and a creature is searching. Find the green exit before it finds you.

Play the game View the code

What you do

Each visit builds a new maze. Halls turn, meet, loop, and end. They look similar on purpose, so it is easy to lose your place. Run if you need to, hide in a dark corner, and press E at the green exit.

The scares stay quiet. A light can go out behind you. A box can shift. A shadow can stand at the end of a hall, then leave if you get close or look away. The creature still walks the maze and catches you if it gets too near.

Controls

Mouse
Look around. Click the screen if the mouse is not locked.
W A S D
Walk.
Shift
Run. The stamina bar drops while you run and fills when you stop.
E
Hide in a dark corner, step back out, or use the exit.
Arrow keys
Turn left and right if the mouse is not locked.

What is in the game

  • Yellow wallpaper, carpet, and ceiling lights.
  • Lights that flicker alone. One can stay off for a few seconds.
  • Fog that makes distant halls darker than the space around you.
  • Rooms, turns, dead ends, and loops.
  • A searching creature, plus a rare shadow that does not chase.
  • Footsteps, a light buzz, creaks, and stretches of silence.
  • A timer. You win at the exit. You lose if the creature catches you.
